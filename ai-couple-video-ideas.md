## Photos that work

- **Two separate photos, one face each.** You don't need a photo of the two of you together.
- **Clear and well lit.** No sunglasses, no group shots, and the face easy to see.
- **Expect new outfits** in the period and dance scenes. These templates keep your faces and hair but dress you for the scene.
- **Only use photos of people who have agreed to it**, and say it is AI-made when you post it.
